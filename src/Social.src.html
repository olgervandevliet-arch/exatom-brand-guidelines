Social media

LinkedIn is the only channel Exatom posts on, so this guide is written for LinkedIn specifically: the profile, the banner, the three post formats, and how a caption sounds. Colors, type and logo rules come from the brand guidelines.

The company page is the one place the mark is seen at 40 px. It has to survive being small and round — and it has to be the exact same file on every account, never a re-export.

Margin
8% of the width on all sides — 96 px on a 1200 px square. Same modular logic as the web and slide margins.
Logo
Bottom-left, height 4% of the frame. Full color on light, white on dark and on the gradient.
Backgrounds
White, blue light, dark, or the gradient. Never a color outside the core palette.
Words on an image
Maximum 12. If it needs more, it is a carousel or it belongs in the caption.
Export
PNG at exact pixel size. LinkedIn recompresses — never upload something already compressed twice.

Carousels

A carousel is a small deck. It uses the presentation layouts at 4:5, uploaded to LinkedIn as a PDF.

Captions

The voice is the one in the tone-of-voice document: bold, challenging, human, driven. On LinkedIn that mostly means starting with the finding, not with a greeting.

First line
The finding, or the number. LinkedIn cuts the caption after roughly 140 characters — the hook has to survive the fold.
Length
80 to 150 words. Short paragraphs, one idea each, a blank line between them.
Numbers
One number carries the post, and it matches the number on the image and in the case study. Never a figure we cannot source.
Hashtags
At most three, at the end, lowercase-readable: #cro #formanalytics #conversionrate
Emoji
None in the body. A single one is allowed to open a hiring or event post, nowhere else.
Links
In the first comment when the post is an image; inline when it is a link card.

Say

"Their address step was losing 54% of visitors." Specific, sourced, and it names the thing that was broken.

Skip

"Excited to share…", "Let's dive in", "game-changing", rhetorical questions as openers, and any claim without a number behind it.
