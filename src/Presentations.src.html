Making a deck in Claude

From an old deck or a few notes to a finished Exatom deck. In a Design, Claude builds each slide as a 1920 × 1080 artboard on a canvas, where you can review and comment.

1

Start with one click

Use Start in Claude or Start in ChatGPT at the top of this page. The prompt makes the AI read the latest rules and the deck kit first. Working another way? Copy the prompt, or attach the two downloaded files.

2

Start a Design or a chat

For slides on a canvas, start a Design in Claude (the Design template, /design). A normal chat works too.

3

Add your material and say what the deck is

An old .pptx or PDF, an outline or meeting notes. For a client deck also the client's logo, a photo of the client, screenshots of their real forms and the dashboard exports you want to show. Say whether it is a sales deck, a pitch or a status meeting, who presents it and to whom.

Rebuild the attached deck as an Exatom presentation. It is a status meeting for [client], presented by [name]. Keep the order and every figure.
4

Review on the canvas

Every slide is its own artboard. Comment on a slide or tell Claude what to change ("take the results wall from the pitch deck", "make the client logo white"). Small text edits you can make directly in the canvas.

5

Share or export

Share the canvas from its Share menu, or export the slides to present. The canvas stays private until you share it.

Real screenshots stay realA client's own forms and Exatom dashboard exports go in as images. Claude only redraws generic product visuals.
No invented figuresClaude takes numbers from your material or the case studies, and leaves [FIGURE] where one is missing.
One file, everyone up to dateA rule that should always hold goes into presentations.md in this site's repository. Push it and within a minute everyone who starts from this page works with it.

The rules at a glance

The short version. The file has the detail.

Format1920 × 1080. 48 px margin, four columns of 432 px with 32 px gutters.
BackgroundsBlue Light for white cards, White for visuals on the blue gradient, Dark for cover and chapters, Blue for the closing, Blush and Burgundy for the problem. No Warm White, no landscapes.
TypeInstrument Sans SemiBold for headings and figures, Inter for text. Chapter 200 px, cover 120, statement 96, slide title 64, body 24.
LabelOne eyebrow per slide, top left, the website's ink-tinted pill.
ImagesReal screenshots are pasted in, generic visuals are rebuilt in brand style. Photos are never cropped awkwardly.
Client decksThe client's logo in white beside ours and their photo on the cover; the presenter's line drawing on the closing slide.
CopyBritish spelling, prospects / buyers / visitors, Smart Nudges and Autofixes, no em dashes. Fixed figures: 87%, 20+, 23%.
