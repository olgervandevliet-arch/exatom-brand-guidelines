Web design

The working reference for how Exatom looks online: logo, color, type, and the interface elements built from them. Every value here is the real brand value — copy from this page, don't approximate. Presentations and social media have their own guides, on their own pages.

Logo

The mark is a rounded square holding the Exatom glyph, paired with the wordmark. Four lockups cover every background; the icon travels alone where space is tight.

Typography

Instrument Sans carries every headline, subhead and button. Inter carries everything you read at length. Two families, no exceptions.

Families

Both load from Google Fonts. Do not substitute other faces in brand communication.

Instrument Sans

Display, headlines, subheads, buttons. Tight tracking at large sizes gives the brand its confident, compact voice.

Foundations

The measurements underneath everything else: how far things sit apart, how sharp their corners are, and where a line is allowed to appear.

Corner radius

Two extremes and little in between: controls are fully pilled, surfaces are close to square. That contrast is what makes a button read as a button.

Buttons & tags999px — Fully pilled — the most recognisable shape in the system
Input fields10px — Softer than surfaces so controls read as interactive
Cards & surfaces6px — Quiet, close to square; the brand is precise, not bubbly
Small elements3–4px — Chips, code, inline marks
Icon badges50% — Only where the shape is the icon itself

Borders & elevation

Separation comes from hairlines, not shadows. One border colour, one weight, everywhere.

Hairline1px solid #F7F4F2 — the only border in the system, for cards, dividers and section breaks.
Input border1px solid #D1D0D5 — the one exception, so a field reads as an editable control.
ElevationNone. No drop shadows anywhere — depth comes from the gradients and from surface colour, not from blur.
Focus0 0 0 3px rgba(42, 90, 233, 0.14) with a Blue border — the single ring in the system.
