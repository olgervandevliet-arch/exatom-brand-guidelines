Presentations

How an Exatom deck is built: the frame, the margins, the eight layouts, and how numbers are shown. Written as a specification, so it holds whether the deck is made in Claude Design or PowerPoint. Colors, type and logo rules come from the brand guidelines — this page only shows how they land on a slide.

Format & margins

One frame, one margin rule. Everything on a slide sits inside the safe area — nothing bleeds to the edge except a full-frame background.

Slide size
1920 × 1080 px — 16:9. The only aspect ratio Exatom presents in.
Margin
76.8 px — longest side ÷ 25, the same modular rule the web guide uses. Round to 77 px where a tool needs whole pixels.
Safe area
1766.4 × 926.4 px. Text, logos and charts stay inside it.
Backgrounds
White, warm white, blue light, dark, or the brand gradient. One background per slide — never a gradient behind a chart.
Export
PDF for sending, 1920 × 1080 PNG per slide for embedding. Never screenshot a slide from the editor.

The blue line is the margin. Backgrounds may cross it; content may not.

Type on slides

The same three families as the web, at slide distance. Sizes below are for a 1920 × 1080 frame.

Cover title
Instrument Sans Semibold · 120 px · −4% letter-spacing · line-height 1.0 · max 10 words
Slide title
Instrument Sans Semibold · 72 px · −3.5% · line-height 1.05 · max 2 lines
Statement
Instrument Sans Semibold · 96 px · −4% · line-height 1.05 · one sentence
Metric
Instrument Sans Semibold · 200 px · −4% · line-height 1.0
Body
Inter Regular · 28 px · line-height 1.5 · max 60 characters per line
Caption & source
Inter Regular · 20 px · gray 500
Eyebrow
Inter Semibold · 20 px · +10% letter-spacing · blue on light, 70% white on dark

Never on a slide

Full-caps titles. Weights above semibold. A fourth typeface. Body text below 24 px. More than six lines of copy. Bullet points three levels deep.

One idea per slide

If a slide needs a title, three bullets and a chart, it is three slides. Decks are read at a distance and skimmed afterwards as a PDF.

Bar color
Blue light for context, blue for the one bar the slide is about. Dark only when the chart sits on a light tint and needs more contrast.
Gridlines
None. Put the value above the bar instead.
Axes
Category labels only. No y-axis, no tick marks, no box around the plot.
Legends
Avoid. Label the series directly where it sits.
Source
Every chart carries a source line in 20 px gray 500 under it. A number without a source does not go on a slide.
Rounding
Whole percentages. One number is the headline; the rest support it.

Building a deck

Decks are made two ways at Exatom. The spec is the same; the mechanics differ.

Claude Design

One artboard per slide, 1920 × 1080, print: "fixed" so Export PDF gives one page per slide. Lay the slides left to right in reading order with 120 px between them. Fonts load from Google Fonts in the helmet block.

PowerPoint

Build the seven layouts as slide masters, not as copied slides. Set the margin guides at 76.8 px on all four sides. Install Instrument Sans and Inter locally before starting, otherwise PowerPoint silently substitutes.
