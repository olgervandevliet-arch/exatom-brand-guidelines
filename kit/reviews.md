**"Deep insights into form behavior combined with powerful session replay"**

What do you like best about Exatom?

What I like most about Exatom is the level of detail it provides on form usage. Instead of only looking at forms on a high level, Exatom allows you to analyze interaction per individual field. You can clearly see how long users take to complete fields, where they run into issues, how often errors occur, and which error messages are triggered.

This makes it possible to optimize forms very precisely based on real data and user behavior. Combined with session replay, Exatom becomes even more powerful. Data alone doesn’t always tell the full story, but being able to watch what actually goes wrong in a session helps you draw better insights and turn them into concrete improvements.

What do you dislike about Exatom?

Exatom is a fairly extensive tool, which means that at times it can take some searching to find the right data or determine which insights to focus on. The onboarding and academy are clear and helpful, but translating all available data into the right conclusions sometimes still requires experience.

That said, this is largely offset by the strong support from the Customer Success Manager, who is very helpful when questions arise.

What problems is Exatom solving and how is that benefiting you?

Exatom helps identify friction and errors in forms that directly impact conversion and user experience. By combining quantitative data with session replay, it becomes much easier to understand why users struggle, where they drop off, and how forms can be improved. This leads to better-performing forms, clearer error handling, and a smoother overall user journey.
